APS Manufatura e Comércio de Filtros Ltda

Manufacturing quality filter elements since 1981

Company

APS Filtros was established in 1981, initially to manufacture and market "membrane-type" filter elements for the agricultural sector (milk processing control - laboratory / lactofiltration / soluble coffee and sugarcane molasses analysis), paints and varnishes (in press filter / sparkler equipment), later expanding to serve other disposable product segments as well as distribution of double-sided cotton tape.

Products - Agricultural

Dairy chain and laboratory analysis

Lentini Disc - Disleite

Discs for milk analysis, soluble coffee or other liquids. Boxes with 100 units of "APS" type - DISLEITE brand - Ø=31.7 mm or 1 ¼". Application: minit filter for milk lactofiltration and analysis of soluble coffee, sugarcane molasses and others.

Filter Sleeves

Sleeve-type filters, sewn. Boxes or packages with 100 pieces in various dimensions. Application: mechanical milking machines and others.

Products - Industrial

Filtration for paint, varnish, and vegetable oil industries

Resin Clarification

Paint industry chain for resin clarification, used in sparkler-type filters.

Circular Filter Membranes

Filter paper or non-woven fabric (industrial filters). Boxes and/or packages with 100 pieces, used in sparkler or press-type filters. Application: filtration of resins and varnishes in paint and varnish industries and/or filtration and clarification of liquids in vegetable oil industries and others.

Double-Sided Tape

Distribution of yellow cotton double-sided "strong" tape, various sizes available upon request, with 30.00 m length for diverse applications such as hair extensions.
